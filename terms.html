English summary: Roku Nana Clip (RokuNanaClip) is an internal desktop tool reserved for our team and not offered to the public. By using it, team members agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, the Meta terms and the TikTok Terms of Service, and must follow each platform's community guidelines and respect third-party rights. Contact: rokunanaclip@gmail.com.
